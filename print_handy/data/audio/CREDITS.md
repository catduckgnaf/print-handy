# Sound credits

## pole_position_nights.mp3

"Pole Position Nights" by Preston Brown
([prestonbrown](https://github.com/prestonbrown)), from HelixScreen
(<https://github.com/prestonbrown/helixscreen>, `assets/sounds/pole_position_nights.mod`).
Licensed CC BY 4.0, as stated by the author:
<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>

This file is an MP3 render of that 4-channel ProTracker module (about
2:08), used unchanged as the default music bed of Pasta Wrapped videos
and the pasta reel.

The module's V10 engine sample is cut from "Red Bull-Cosworth RB1
(2005).ogg" by Edvvc, Wikimedia Commons, licensed CC BY-SA 3.0:
<https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Red_Bull-Cosworth_RB1_(2005).ogg>
<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/>
The sample is 15.5-24.5 s of the recording, high-passed, slowed to 80%
tempo at constant pitch, resampled to 11084 Hz and faded in and out
(per HelixScreen's `assets/sounds/CREDITS.md`).

Any other sample added here must be public domain, or be listed in this
file with its source and a freely distributable license.
